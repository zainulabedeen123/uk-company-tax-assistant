Scope: Corporation Tax financial year 2026 (FY26: 1 Apr 2026–31 Mar 2027); tax year 2026/27 (6 Apr 2026–5 Apr 2027).

## Corporation Tax (HMRC: "Corporation Tax rates and allowances")

| Item | FY2026 (also FY2023–FY2025) |
|---|---|
| Small profits rate (augmented profits ≤ lower limit) | 19% |
| Main rate (augmented profits > upper limit) | 25% |
| Marginal relief lower limit | £50,000 |
| Marginal relief upper limit | £250,000 |
| Standard fraction | 3/200 |
| Effective marginal rate on profits between the limits | 26.5% |
| Ring fence (oil) small / main rate | 19% / 30%, fraction 11/400 |

- Limits are divided by (1 + number of associated companies) and pro-rated for accounting periods shorter than 12 months (days in period / 365).
- Quarterly instalment payments (QIPs): "large" if augmented profits > £1.5m; "very large" if > £20m (both thresholds divided by 1 + associated companies; pro-rated for short periods).
- Late filing penalties (returns with a filing date on or after 1 April 2026 — doubled by Budget 2025):

| Failure | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Return 1 day late | £200 |
| Return more than 3 months late | further £200 |
| Third successive late return — 1 day late | £1,000 |
| Third successive late return — more than 3 months late | further £1,000 (total £2,000) |
| 6 months late | HMRC determination + 10% of unpaid tax |
| 12 months late | further 10% of unpaid tax |

(Before 1 April 2026 filing dates: £100 / £200 / £500 / £1,000.)

- Late payment interest: Bank Rate + 4% (since 6 April 2025). Bank Rate was 3.75% at the September 2026 MPC, giving 7.75% — check HMRC's current interest rates page as Bank Rate changes. Repayment interest: Bank Rate − 1% (minimum 0.5%). Interest is deductible for Corporation Tax (loan relationship debit).

## Income tax 2026/27 (rest of UK — England, Wales, NI)

| Band | Taxable income | Rate | Dividend rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal Allowance | £0–£12,570 | 0% | 0% |
| Basic | £12,571–£50,270 | 20% | **10.75%** |
| Higher | £50,271–£125,140 | 40% | **35.75%** |
| Additional | over £125,140 | 45% | 39.35% |

- Dividend rates rose by 2 points on 6 April 2026 (were 8.75% / 33.75% / 39.35%).
- Dividend Allowance £500. Personal Savings Allowance £1,000 basic / £500 higher / £0 additional. Starting rate for savings £5,000.
- Personal Allowance reduced by £1 for every £2 of adjusted net income over £100,000 (nil at £125,140). Personal Allowance and higher-rate threshold frozen until April 2031.
- High Income Child Benefit Charge: 1% per £200 of adjusted net income over £60,000; full clawback at £80,000.
- Marriage Allowance transfer £1,260.

### Scotland 2026/27 (non-savings, non-dividend income only)

Starter 19% to £3,967 above PA; basic 20% £3,968–£16,956; intermediate 21% £16,957–£31,092; higher 42% £31,093–£62,430; advanced 45% £62,431–£125,140; top 48% above £125,140. Dividends and savings use UK rates and bands.

## National Insurance 2026/27

| Item | Annual | Monthly | Weekly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower Earnings Limit (LEL) | £6,708 | £559 | £129 |
| Primary threshold (employee) | £12,570 | £1,048 | £242 |
| Secondary threshold (employer) | £5,000 | £417 | £96 |
| Upper Earnings Limit / upper secondary thresholds (under 21, apprentice under 25, veterans) | £50,270 | £4,189 | £967 |

- Employee Class 1: 8% between PT and UEL; 2% above UEL.
- Employer Class 1: 15% above ST. Class 1A and 1B: 15%.
- Employment Allowance: £10,500 per year (not available to a company whose only employee paid above the secondary threshold is a director).
- Apprenticeship Levy: 0.5% of pay bill over £3m (allowance £15,000).
- Directors use an annual earnings period for NIC.

## Other payroll figures 2026/27

- National Living Wage (21+): £12.71/hour; 18–20: £10.85; under 18 and apprentices: £8.00 (from 1 April 2026).
- Statutory Sick Pay: £123.25/week or 80% of AWE if lower.
- SMP/SPP/SAP/ShPP standard weekly rate: £194.32 or 90% of AWE if lower.
- Approved mileage (own car): 55p/mile first 10,000 business miles, 25p thereafter (55p for all miles for NIC); motorcycle 24p; cycle 20p. (Car rate raised from 45p in June 2026.)
- Student loan thresholds: Plan 1 £26,900; Plan 2 £29,385; Plan 4 £33,795; Plan 5 £25,000; postgraduate £21,000 (9% / 6%).
- Trivial benefits: up to £50 per benefit; directors/office-holders of close companies capped at £300 per tax year.
- Official rate of interest (beneficial loans): **[verify for 2026/27 on GOV.UK "official rate of interest"]**. Beneficial loan threshold £10,000.

## Directors' loans

- s455 tax on loans to participators not repaid within 9 months and 1 day of the period end: **35.75%** for loans made on or after 6 April 2026 (33.75% for loans made 6 April 2022–5 April 2026; 32.5% before).

## Capital gains (individuals) 2026/27

- CGT rates 18% (basic) / 24% (higher). Annual exempt amount £3,000.
- Business Asset Disposal Relief: 18% from 6 April 2026 (14% in 2025/26), lifetime limit £1m.
- Investors' Relief: 18%, lifetime limit £1m.

## VAT

- Standard 20%; reduced 5%; zero 0%.
- Registration threshold £90,000 (rolling 12 months or next 30 days). Deregistration threshold £88,000.
- Flat Rate Scheme joining limit £150,000 (excl. VAT); limited cost trader rate 16.5%.
- Cash Accounting and Annual Accounting: join if taxable turnover ≤ £1.35m; leave cash accounting above £1.6m.

## R&D (accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024)

- Merged scheme: 20% taxable expenditure credit (net benefit 15%–16.2% depending on tax rate).
- Enhanced R&D Intensive Support (ERIS) for loss-making SMEs with R&D intensity ≥ 30%: 86% additional deduction + 14.5% payable credit.
- Claim notification within 6 months of period end for first-time/lapsed claimants; Additional Information Form must be submitted before the CT600 claim.
