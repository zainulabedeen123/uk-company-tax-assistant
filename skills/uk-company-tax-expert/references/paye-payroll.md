# PAYE, National Insurance and payroll

Legislation: ITEPA 2003, Income Tax (PAYE) Regulations 2003, SSCBA 1992, Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, Pensions Act 2008 (auto-enrolment). Figures: `rates-2026-27.md`.

## 1. When to register as an employer

Register with HMRC **before the first payday** (can register up to 2 months before) if paying anyone — including a director — at or above the Lower Earnings Limit (£129/week), or who has another job/pension, or who receives expenses/benefits. A director-only company paying a salary below the LEL with no other employees does not strictly need a PAYE scheme, but most directors register to record salary properly. Registration gives an employer PAYE reference and Accounts Office reference.

## 2. Real Time Information (RTI)

| Submission | When |
|---|---|
| Full Payment Submission (FPS) | On or before every payday |
| Employer Payment Summary (EPS) | By the 19th after the end of the tax month (to claim Employment Allowance, statutory pay recovery, report no payments, CIS deductions suffered) |
| Pay HMRC (PAYE, NIC, student loans) | By the **22nd** of the following tax month if paid electronically (19th by cheque). Tax months run 6th–5th |
| Quarterly payments | Allowed if average monthly payments < £1,500 — due 22 July, 22 Oct, 22 Jan, 22 Apr |
| P60 to employees | By 31 May |
| P11D and P11D(b) (benefits and Class 1A) | By 6 July after the tax year |
| Pay Class 1A NIC | By 22 July (electronic) |
| PAYE Settlement Agreement | Agree by 5 July; pay tax and Class 1B by 22 October |
| P45 | When an employee leaves |

Payroll software must be HMRC-recognised (free options exist for fewer than 10 employees — HMRC's Basic PAYE Tools was free but check availability). Payrolling of benefits: voluntary now; mandatory payrolling of most benefits planned from April 2027 **[verify]**.

## 3. Penalties

- Late FPS: monthly penalties from £100 (1–9 employees) to £400 (250+), first failure in a tax year excused. Additional 5% if more than 3 months late.
- Late payment: escalating penalty 1%–4% depending on number of defaults in the tax year; 5% more at 6 and 12 months.
- Late P11D: £100 per 50 employees per month.

## 4. National Insurance for directors

- Directors have an **annual earnings period**: NIC calculated on cumulative earnings for the year (or pro-rata if appointed mid-year), rather than per pay period. Payroll software usually offers the "alternative arrangements" (pay-period basis with year-end adjustment).
- Salary between LEL (£6,708) and PT (£12,570) gives a qualifying year for state pension at zero employee NIC.
- Employer NIC 15% above £5,000.

## 5. Employment Allowance (£10,500 for 2026/27)

- Reduces employer Class 1 NIC. Claim via EPS each tax year.
- **Not available** where the only employee paid above the secondary threshold is a director (single-director companies). A company with two directors both paid above £5,000 can claim. Also excluded: public bodies (mostly), employees for personal/household work, companies where deemed payments under IR35 are the only ones.
- Connected companies: only one company in the group can claim.

## 6. Benefits in kind and expenses

- Report taxable benefits on P11D (or payroll them); employer pays Class 1A at 15%.
- Exempt: employer pension contributions, one mobile phone, trivial benefits (≤ £50 each; director cap £300/year), annual staff party (≤ £150 per head), workplace parking, cycle-to-work, eye tests, health screening, certain home-working equipment, business mileage paid at or below approved rates (55p/25p cars).
- Company cars: benefit = list price × appropriate percentage (by CO2; electric cars 4% in 2026/27, rising 1 point a year to 2027/28 then 2 points a year from 2028/29 **[verify]**); fuel benefit uses the fuel multiplier.
- Beneficial loans > £10,000: benefit at the official rate of interest less interest paid.
- Salary sacrifice: only exempt for pensions, childcare, cycle-to-work, ultra-low emission cars, pension advice; otherwise optional remuneration rules (OpRA) apply. Pension salary-sacrifice NIC cap of £2,000 a year announced for April 2029 **[verify]**.

## 7. Workplace pensions (auto-enrolment)

- Duties start on the first employee's start date. Assess workers: eligible jobholders (age 22 to state pension age, earnings > £10,000) must be auto-enrolled. Minimum contribution 8% of qualifying earnings (employer ≥ 3%), qualifying earnings band £6,240–£50,270 **[verify 2026/27 band]**.
- Declaration of compliance to The Pensions Regulator within 5 months of the duties start date; re-enrolment every 3 years.
- **Exemption**: companies whose only staff are directors without employment contracts, or a single director with a contract — tell TPR no duties.

## 8. National Minimum Wage

Applies to workers and employees. Directors who are office-holders without a contract of employment are not entitled to NMW, which is why low director salaries are lawful.

## 9. Off-payroll working (IR35)

- Small client companies (meeting 2 of: turnover ≤ £15m, balance sheet ≤ £7.5m, ≤ 50 employees — thresholds updated April 2025 with the Companies Act size changes **[verify commencement]**): the contractor's own personal service company decides status and, if inside IR35, calculates a deemed employment payment at year end (Chapter 8 ITEPA).
- Medium/large clients: client issues a Status Determination Statement; fee-payer deducts PAYE (Chapter 10).
- Escalate disputed status cases.
