# VAT for UK companies

Legislation: Value Added Tax Act 1994 (VATA), VAT Regulations 1995. Guidance: VAT Notice 700 (general), 700/1 (registration), 733 (Flat Rate Scheme), 731 (cash accounting), 732 (annual accounting), 700/22 (Making Tax Digital), 741A (place of supply of services). Figures: `rates-2026-27.md`.

## 1. Registration

- **Compulsory**: taxable turnover (standard, reduced and zero-rated sales — not exempt or outside scope) in the last 12 months exceeds £90,000 → register within 30 days of the end of the month in which the threshold was exceeded; effective date = first day of the second month after. Or expected to exceed £90,000 in the next 30 days alone → register by the end of those 30 days; effective from the start of that period.
- Non-established taxable persons (overseas businesses supplying in the UK): register from the first taxable supply, no threshold.
- **Voluntary registration** below the threshold: recover input VAT; useful for B2B businesses.
- **Exception** if the threshold is exceeded only temporarily (next 12 months expected ≤ £88,000). **Exemption from registration** if supplies are mainly zero-rated.
- Late registration: VAT due on all sales from the date registration should have taken effect + failure to notify penalty.
- Register online via the HMRC business tax account (Government Gateway). VAT number arrives with the effective date of registration.
- **Pre-registration input VAT**: goods still held at registration bought within 4 years; services within 6 months before registration.
- Deregister if taxable turnover expected to fall below £88,000 in the next 12 months, or must deregister if stopping taxable supplies (within 30 days). Output VAT on assets held at deregistration if VAT > £1,000.

## 2. Rates and liability

- Standard 20%; reduced 5% (e.g. domestic fuel and power, children's car seats, some energy-saving materials); zero 0% (most food, books and newspapers, children's clothes, exports of goods, new residential construction).
- Exempt (no VAT, no input recovery): most financial services, insurance, education/health by qualifying bodies, residential lets, land (unless opted to tax).
- Outside scope: wages, dividends, statutory fees, donations without benefit, most supplies of services to overseas business customers (place of supply outside UK — B2B general rule, VATA s7A).
- Reverse charge: services received from overseas suppliers (B2B), domestic reverse charge for construction (CIS) services, specified goods (mobile phones, chips) and wholesale energy.
- Imports: postponed VAT accounting on the VAT return. Exports of goods: zero-rated with evidence.

## 3. Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT

All VAT-registered businesses must keep digital records and file returns using MTD-compatible software (spreadsheet + bridging software allowed). Digital links required between records and the return — no manual copy-paste between software products.

## 4. Returns and payments

- Standard: quarterly returns (stagger chosen by HMRC); monthly option for repayment traders.
- Deadline: return **and** payment due **1 calendar month and 7 days** after the end of the VAT period (direct debit taken 3 working days later).
- Payments on account required if VAT liability > £2.3m a year.
- Nine boxes on the VAT return: Box 1 output VAT on sales; Box 2 VAT due on acquisitions from EU (NI goods only); Box 3 total VAT due; Box 4 input VAT reclaimed; Box 5 net VAT; Box 6 total sales ex VAT (incl. zero-rated/exempt); Box 7 total purchases ex VAT; Box 8 goods supplied to EU (NI only); Box 9 goods acquired from EU (NI only).
- Errors: correct on the next return if net error ≤ £10,000, or up to 1% of Box 6 (max £50,000); otherwise disclose on form VAT652. 4-year time limit.

## 5. Penalties (VAT periods from 1 January 2023)

- **Late submission — points-based**: 1 point per late return; penalty £200 when reaching the threshold (annual returns 2 points, quarterly 4, monthly 5) and £200 for each further late return. Points expire after a compliance period (quarterly: 12 months of on-time returns and all returns in the previous 24 months submitted).
- **Late payment** (from April 2025 rates): nothing if paid within 15 days; 3% of tax outstanding at day 15; further 3% of tax outstanding at day 30; then daily penalty at 10% a year from day 31 until paid. Agreeing a Time to Pay arrangement stops further penalties accruing.
- Late payment interest: Bank Rate + 4%.
- Inaccuracy penalties as for other taxes (careless/deliberate).

## 6. Schemes

- **Flat Rate Scheme** (join if expected taxable turnover ≤ £150,000; leave if total income > £230,000): pay a fixed percentage of gross (VAT-inclusive) turnover; no input recovery except capital goods > £2,000. 1% discount in the first year. **Limited cost trader** (goods < 2% of turnover or < £1,000 a year): 16.5% — usually makes FRS poor value for service businesses.
- **Cash Accounting**: account for VAT when paid/received; join ≤ £1.35m, leave > £1.6m. Bad-debt protection.
- **Annual Accounting**: one return a year, 9 monthly or 3 quarterly interim payments; join ≤ £1.35m.
- Margin schemes (second-hand goods), Tour Operators' Margin Scheme, retail schemes.

## 7. Input VAT recovery rules

- Need a valid VAT invoice (simplified invoices ≤ £250).
- Blocked: business entertainment of non-employees, cars available for private use (50% on leased cars), goods/services for private or exempt use (partial exemption de minimis: exempt input tax ≤ £625/month average and ≤ 50% of total input tax).
- Fuel for private use: fuel scale charge or claim business mileage only.
- Bad-debt relief: debts over 6 months old and written off.

## 8. Records

Keep for 6 years: VAT account, sales and purchase invoices, digital records under MTD.
